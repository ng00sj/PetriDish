模擬使用之偏微分方程式 ▼
1. 菌株生物量演化方程式 ($b_i$)：
$$\frac{\partial b_i}{\partial t} = \underbrace{D_b^{\text{eff}} \nabla^2 b_i}_{\text{濕度調節擴散}} - \underbrace{\nabla \cdot \left[\chi\, b_i \left(1 - \frac{b_{\text{tot}}}{K}\right)_+ \nabla b_{\text{tot}}\right]}_{\text{守恆體積填充聚集}} + \underbrace{\nabla \cdot \left(M_{\text{ch}} \frac{b_i}{b_{\text{tot}}} \nabla \mu_{\text{ch}}\right)}_{\text{Cahn-Hilliard 相分離}} + \underbrace{\mu(c)\, E(x,y)\, b_i \left(1 - \frac{b_{\text{tot}}}{K}\right)_+ \left(1 + \sigma_\eta\sqrt{\Delta t}\,\eta_i\right)}_{\text{局部環境加權隨機生長}} - \underbrace{d\big(1 - E(x,y)\big)\,b_i}_{\text{逆境衰亡}}$$
2. 局部酸鹼度 ($\text{pH}$) 與溶氧場 ($O_2$) 微環境演化：
$$\frac{\partial (\text{pH})}{\partial t} = \underbrace{D_{\text{pH}} \nabla^2 (\text{pH})}_{\text{離子擴散}} + \underbrace{\alpha_{\text{pH}} \, \frac{c}{K_c + c} \, b_{\text{tot}}}_{\text{代謝產酸/產鹼}} + \underbrace{\beta_{\text{buf}} (\text{pH}_{\text{env}} - \text{pH})}_{\text{緩衝液平衡}}$$ $$\frac{\partial O_2}{\partial t} = \underbrace{D_{O_2} \nabla^2 O_2}_{\text{氧氣擴散}} - \underbrace{q_{O_2} \, \frac{c}{K_c + c} \frac{O_2}{K_{O_2} + O_2} \, b_{\text{tot}}}_{\text{好氧呼吸消耗}} + \underbrace{k_{La} (O_{2,\text{env}} - O_2)}_{\text{空氣溶氧補充}}$$
3. Cahn-Hilliard 化學勢 ($\mu_{\text{ch}}$) 與局部環境適應度 ($E$)：
$$\mu_{\text{ch}} = \underbrace{2\phi(1-\phi)(1-2\phi)}_{\text{雙井自由能導數 } f'(\phi)} - \underbrace{\kappa_{\text{ch}}\nabla^2\phi}_{\text{相界表面張力}}, \qquad E(x,y) = \underbrace{f_T(T)}_{\text{溫度}} \cdot \underbrace{f_{\text{pH}}\big(\text{pH}(x,y)\big)}_{\text{局部酸鹼}} \cdot \underbrace{f_{O_2}\big(O_2(x,y)\big)}_{\text{局部含氧}} \cdot \underbrace{f_H(H)}_{\text{濕度}}$$
3. Cahn-Hilliard 化學勢 ($\mu_{\text{ch}}$) 與局部環境適應度 ($E$)：
$$\mu_{\text{ch}} = \underbrace{2\phi(1-\phi)(1-2\phi)}_{\text{雙井自由能導數 } f'(\phi)} - \underbrace{\kappa_{\text{ch}}\nabla^2\phi}_{\text{相界表面張力}}, \qquad E(x,y) = \underbrace{f_T(T)}_{\text{溫度}} \cdot \underbrace{f_{\text{pH}}\big(\text{pH}(x,y)\big)}_{\text{局部酸鹼}} \cdot \underbrace{f_{O_2}(O_2)}_{\text{含氧}} \cdot \underbrace{f_H(H)}_{\text{濕度}}$$
4. 營養鹽濃度場演化方程式 ($c$)：
$$\frac{\partial c}{\partial t} = \underbrace{D_c \nabla^2 c}_{\text{營養擴散}} - \underbrace{q\,\mu(c)\,E(x,y)\,b_{\text{tot}}}_{\text{環境加權消耗}} + \underbrace{s(1 - c)}_{\text{基質補充}}, \qquad \mu(c) = \frac{\mu_{\max}\,c}{K_c + c}$$
